# Appointment prep

## Goal

Build a one-page agenda from verified records.

Prepare questions. Leave diagnosis and treatment decisions to the clinical team.

## Inputs

Ask permission before opening each file.

Use:

- the latest verified `00-current-state.md`;
- changes since the last appointment;
- the side-effect log;
- open decision notes;
- the last clinician note, when approved.

Skip `care-team.md` unless contact details are needed for the final document.

## Verification

- Use source IDs for every clinical fact.
- Copy doses, units, and dates exactly.
- Mark missing or conflicting information.
- Keep memory-based notes labeled `UNVERIFIED`.
- Never invent a question from an unsupported claim.

## Hard rules

Keep it to one page when possible. Rank by urgency stated by the patient or clinician. Do not infer urgency from the record.

If the person reports urgent or rapidly worsening symptoms, stop. Direct them to the treating team or local emergency service.
